Import from Samsung Health

Samsung Health doesn't connect to web pages, so workouts come in as its export file. Rides that match a bike ride here (within 15 minutes) pick up the watch's heart rate and calories.

  1. On your phone, open Samsung Health → ⋮ menu → Settings → Download personal data.
  2. Open the folder it saves (usually Download/Samsung Health/…).
  3. Pick the file named com.samsung.shealth.exercise.…csv below.

Live heart rate from the watch

Galaxy Watch doesn't broadcast heart rate on its own. Install a broadcaster app on the watch, start it, then tap Connect heart rate above.

  1. Install Heart for Bluetooth (or a similar app) on the watch.
  2. Start broadcasting on the watch before your ride.
  3. The IC4's included armband works too: same button.

Bluetooth needs Chrome or Edge on Android, Windows, Mac or ChromeOS. iPhone and iPad browsers can't connect, but logging and import still work there.

Paste rides
